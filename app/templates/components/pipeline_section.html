Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Security, compliance, and algorithmic methodology

How does Aura Humanizer ensure my text passes Turnitin and GPTZero?

Turnitin and GPTZero do not look for plagiarism in AI text; they look for Perplexity (word choice probability) and Burstiness (sentence length variance). Aura explicitly breaks both mathematical invariants by injecting irregular clause lengths, active voice shifts, and high-entropy synonym substitutions.

Are my submitted drafts and API keys stored on any database?

No. Aura utilizes a strict Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture. Keys and text are stored exclusively in your browser's private LocalStorage and processed via stateless in-memory streams. Zero text retention.

Does the synthesizer preserve technical terms and academic citations?

Yes. In "Academic" mode, the rule engine specifically protects parenthetical citations, standard formatting (APA, MLA, Chicago), mathematical terms, and domain-specific terminology while transforming connective syntax.
